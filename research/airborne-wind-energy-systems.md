# Airborne Wind Energy Systems

## Flight dynamics and experimental co-design

Tethered wings and buoyant lifting bodies access high-altitude winds. CORE investigates their dynamics and jointly optimizes physical designs and controllers using scaled water-channel experiments. Historical work includes roughly 1/100-scale models and comparisons with Altaeros flight tests.

## Wind mapping and altitude selection

Controllers must learn the wind profile while selecting productive operating altitudes. This creates competing information-gathering and energy-harvesting objectives, addressed through extremum seeking and related optimization methods.

## Crosswind energy harvesting

Repeated crosswind trajectories can increase wing speed and power generation. The existing site records lab-scale demonstrations beginning in March 2016 and research into trajectory optimization across changing flow conditions.

## Collaboration and support

The original page describes collaboration with Altaeros and NSF support for experimental optimization, spatiotemporal control, and economic iterative learning.
