# Renewably Powered Robotic Systems

## WEC-Mobile

Wave Glider motion powers pumping and reverse-osmosis desalination. Control work coordinates pump sizing, valve adjustment, and routes chosen from sea-state forecasts.

## SSailOR

The Spherical Sailing Omnidirectional Rover uses sails, a traction hoop, ballast, and a protective cage. Co-design balances heel, rolling resistance, mobility, and disturbance sensitivity.

## Foiling solar-powered vessels

The source describes a preliminary hydrofoil ASV design targeting faster sustainable transit and support for launching, recovering, and recharging other vehicles. These are design objectives.

## Cyber-physical oceanography

Energy-aware mission planning coordinates a renewable-powered host vessel and rechargeable satellite vehicles. Clarity measures information; planning balances observations, energy, and rendezvous. The source reports SP-48 and aerial-vehicle experiments.

## Persistent mission planning

Renewable resources make mobility uncertain. Work includes stochastic sailing-route optimization and predictive planning for solar-powered vessels with energy storage.
