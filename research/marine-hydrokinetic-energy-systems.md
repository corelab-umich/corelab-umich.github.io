# Marine Hydrokinetic Energy Systems

## Underwater energy-harvesting kites

CORE studies tethered kites that move across ocean or tidal currents to increase power capture. Research combines physical design, control, and scaled experiments in water channels, pools, and open-water tow tests, including testing at Michigan's Marine Hydrodynamics Lab. The Duct-Sail concept combines a high-lift duct with kite motion.

## Wave-powered desalination

The WEC-Mobile uses Wave Glider motion to drive a pump and reverse-osmosis unit. Control research addresses component sizing, valve settings, and mission planning.

## Ocean-current turbine arrays

Parameterized geometric patterns reduce the complexity of optimizing three-dimensional turbine spacing and layout.

## Relocatable underwater vehicles

An energy-harvesting AUV can select mooring locations to follow a changing Gulf Stream resource. Work combines environmental modeling, vehicle design, and navigation control.

## Collaboration and support

The page lists NC State, Maryland, the Coastal Studies Institute, and Florida Atlantic collaborators, with support from DOE, the DARPA Manta Ray program, and North Carolina ocean-energy programs.
