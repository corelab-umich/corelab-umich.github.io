# Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

## Adaptable vehicle design

CORE investigates set-based digital engineering for highly automated off-road vehicles. A vehicle's design can retain a range of configurations at deployment, enabling changes in hardware settings and control as conditions evolve. Examples include modular components, adjustable suspension, and adaptive software.

## Human–vehicle shared control

Game-theoretic models and iterative learning help tailor the blend of driver and autonomous inputs to individual behavior and route segments. Work described on the site explores cognitive hierarchy, gradient methods, and Bayesian optimization, with Army Automotive Research Center support.

## Predictive control for fuel economy

Historical collaboration with Penn State, MIT, and Volvo optimized routing, speed, transmission, and engine/accessory control. The program targeted a 20% fuel-economy improvement and included simulation and experiments with a Volvo VNL 300 diesel truck. That percentage is a program goal, not a claimed achieved result.
