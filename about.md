# About CORE Lab

CORE is the **Control and Optimization for Renewables and Energy Efficiency Lab** at the University of Michigan. The group applies optimization and advanced control to renewable energy harvesting and energy-efficient systems.

Its methods include hierarchical control, model predictive control, economic iterative learning control, and machine learning for online control. Applications span tethered wind energy, marine energy, renewably powered robots, and connected and autonomous vehicles.
