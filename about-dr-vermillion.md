# Chris Vermillion

**Associate Professor, Mechanical Engineering · University of Michigan**

Chris Vermillion applies optimal control and design optimization to renewable energy and energy efficiency. His research includes tethered wind and marine energy, economic iterative learning, experimental co-design, and hierarchical model predictive control for vehicles.

He earned his degrees at Michigan, then worked at the Toyota Technical Center and as a lead engineer at Altaeros. He established CORE at UNC Charlotte and later served at NC State before returning to Michigan.

## Education

- Ph.D., Electrical Engineering, University of Michigan, 2009.
- M.S., Electrical Engineering, University of Michigan, 2005.
- B.S.E., Mechanical Engineering, University of Michigan, 2004.
- B.S.E., Aerospace Engineering, University of Michigan, 2004.

## Selected recognition

The faculty directory lists the NSF CAREER Award (2015), Rudolf E. Kalman Best Paper Award (2019), NC State research award (2021), and North Carolina Renewable Ocean Energy Program Edge Award (2022).
