Students

Current Students:

Minhazul Alam, Ph.D. Student 

Contact: minhaz@umich.edu

Minhazul Alam is a Ph.D. student in the CORE Lab whose research interests focus on the optimal mission planning of energy-harvesting robotic vessels within a spatiotemporally varying marine environment.

Aleksandra Dudek, Ph.D. Student 

Contact: aadudek@umich.edu

Aleksandra Dudek is a Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan focused on the development of a control framework that leverages game theoretic modeling and iterative learning control to improve human-robot collaboration. She previously completed her Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles. In her free time, Aleksandra enjoys running, reading, and spending time outdoors!

Kavin Govindarajan, Ph.D. Student

Contact: kmgovind@umich.edu

Kavin Govindarajan is a graduate student within the Robotics department at U of M. His research focus is on developing and implementing optimal control algorithms for sustainably powered robotic systems in spatiotemporally varying environments. Specifically, he works on the control of a solar-powered autonomous surface vessel (ASV) for persistent oceanographic observation.

Limeng Liu, Ph.D. Student 

Contact: llimeng@umich.edu

Limeng Liu is a Ph.D. student focused on learning control algorithms for the optimal control of a mobile, anchorless wave-powered desalination platform. Before entering the Ph.D. program, he received MSME and MSME degrees, both in mechanical engineering, from Purdue University. In his free time, he enjoys making tasty food for his friends.

Mary Maceda, Ph.D. Student (NC State) 

Contact: memaceda@ncsu.edu

Mary Maceda is a Ph.D. student at NC State who focuses on the optimization of underwater kite designs and the development of model predictive control (MPC) algorithms for real-time optimization of kite performance. She completed her Bachelor’s degree at Saint Vincent College, where she was a student-athlete. In her free time, Mary enjoys playing softball and ultimate frisbee, in addition to reading books and participating in trivia nights.

Zihan Yu, Ph.D. Student

Contact: zihanyu@umich.edu

Zihan Yu is a Ph.D. student specializing in learning-based techniques for human-centered autonomous systems, with a particular focus on autonomous vehicles and mobile robots. Prior to starting his Ph.D. program, he earned a Bachelor of Engineering degree from Northeastern University (China) and a Master of Philosophy degree from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. In his free time, he enjoys watching soccer and basketball games, reading history books, and traveling.

Ashley Ortenburg, Undergraduate Student

Contact: aorten@umich.edu

Ashley Ortenburg is an undergraduate student studying mechanical engineering at U of M. Her research focus is on the modeling and control of the spherical sailing omnidirectional rover (SSailOR).

Diego Ramirez-Gomez, Undergraduate Student

Contact: ramdiego@umich.edu

Diego Ramirez-Gomez is a senior undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Michigan. His focus is on continuing the development of the spherical sailing omnidirectional rover (SSailOR). In his free time, he enjoys watching football and basketball, as well as going to the gym with friends.
