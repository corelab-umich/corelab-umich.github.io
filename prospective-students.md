# Prospective Students

## Current or accepted graduate students

The existing site asks interested students to email Chris Vermillion with:

1. A two-paragraph statement explaining their research interests and a problem they would like to pursue.
2. A CV that includes undergraduate GPA.
3. A technical writing sample, such as a project report or research paper.

The page describes strong quantitative and writing skills, clear research motivation, communication skills, and typically an undergraduate GPA of at least 3.5 as considerations.

## Prospective graduate applicants

Mention interest in the group's research in the graduate application personal statement.

## Current undergraduate students

Send a two-paragraph statement outlining research interests and a possible project. The original page describes summer research opportunities and the possibility of continuing into graduate study.
